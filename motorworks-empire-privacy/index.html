Privacy Policy Terms of Use

This Privacy Policy explains how Motorworks Empire ("the game", "we") handles information. The game is published by Future Games.

Information stored on your device

You do not need an account. Your progress, settings and language are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Optional cloud save

Cloud save is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, a copy of your game progress (the same text as "Back up progress") is stored in your own account: Google Drive's private app data folder on Android, or the game's private iCloud container on iOS. It is not visible in your Drive or Files app, other apps cannot read it, and we never receive it. On Android the game asks you to sign in with Google and only requests the "app data" permission for this one folder; it cannot see any other file in your Drive. Turn cloud save off in Settings at any time; to delete the stored copy, remove the game's app data in your Google Drive settings or iCloud storage settings.

Backup code

Settings → Back up progress copies a backup code to your clipboard. We do not receive it; it stays wherever you paste it.

Purchases

In-app purchases (for example Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. To deliver an item, the game may verify the purchase receipt (order id and product id) with our verification service. Receipts are kept only as long as needed to prevent fraud and handle refunds.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob. Nothing is shown unless you choose to watch one. Google may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show, measure and protect ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first using Google's consent message (IAB TCF); without consent only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. Buying Remove Ads removes the ad prompts. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash reports

To fix bugs, the game can send anonymous crash reports (the error message and where it happened, the app version and platform, and a random install id generated on your device). This is on by default and you can switch it off in Settings → Send crash reports. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, advertising id or purchase tokens, and the install id cannot be linked to you. The game does not collect usage analytics.

Notifications

Reminders (construction finished, offline production full, daily rewards) are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to family-friendly content. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. Because we keep no personal data on our servers, most requests are handled on your device (ad privacy settings, resetting your advertising id, turning off cloud save, uninstalling). Contact us at futuregames31@gmail.com for anything else. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this page if the game starts processing data in a new way.
