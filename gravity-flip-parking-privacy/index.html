Gravity Flip Parking

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravity Flip Parking ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Gravity Flip Parking is a car parking/driving game. It does not require you to create an account.

  • Game progress: your level progress, coins, gems, unlocked cars and customization items are stored locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.
  • Endless mode leaderboard: if you play Endless Mode and submit a score, we store the nickname you choose and your score in our database so it can appear on the online leaderboard. This nickname is chosen by you and is not linked to your real identity, email, or any account.
  • Advertising identifiers: the app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA) to show and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted by your device settings. On iOS, this only happens if you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Diagnostic data: we use Firebase to support the online leaderboard and basic app stability. Firebase may collect standard technical/diagnostic information such as app version and crash data.

In-App Purchases

Gravity Flip Parking does not currently offer in-app purchases. Extra rewards (such as reviving after a crash, bonus coins, or gems) are unlocked by choosing to watch a rewarded video ad.

Third-Party Services

Gravity Flip Parking uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob - for displaying ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) - for storing and displaying the Endless Mode online leaderboard. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

On iOS, you can allow or deny ad tracking when prompted by App Tracking Transparency, or change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Google Settings. Submitting a leaderboard score and choosing a nickname is entirely optional and only required if you want to appear on the Endless Mode leaderboard.

Data Deletion

Since your game progress is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. To request removal of a leaderboard entry you submitted, contact us at the email below with the nickname and approximate date you submitted it.

Children's Privacy

Gravity Flip Parking is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
