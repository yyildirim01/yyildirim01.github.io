Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Your progress stays on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not sell personal data. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Turn on Settings → Data & privacy → Cloud save on the old phone (Google Drive on Android, iCloud on iPhone), then turn it on in the new phone and choose "Use cloud save". Or use Settings → Back up progress to copy a backup code and paste it with Restore on the new phone.

What does cloud save store?

Only your game progress, in a private slot of your own Google Drive or iCloud that other apps and people cannot see. It is optional and off until you turn it on. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Cloud save says "Couldn't reach the cloud".

Check your internet connection and that you are signed in to Google (Android) or iCloud (iPhone, Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive on). Then try again.

My progress is gone after restoring.

When two saves exist, the game asks which one to keep and shows the level and coins of both. If you picked the wrong one, turn cloud save on again and choose the other copy; contact us if you need help.

How do I remove ads?

Open the Shop and buy Remove Ads once. It is a lifetime purchase and rewards from ad buttons are then granted instantly, without watching a video. Ads are always optional rewarded videos.

How do I restore a purchase I already made?

Use "Restore purchases" in the Shop, signed in with the same Google account or Apple ID you bought with.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Your factory keeps producing and you can play without internet. Rewarded ads, cloud save and purchases need a connection.

How do I turn off reminders or crash reports?

Settings has switches for reminders and for sending crash reports. Reminders can also be turned off in your phone's system settings.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game to remove local data. If you used cloud save, also remove the game's app data in Google Drive settings (Manage apps) or iCloud storage settings.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us with as much detail as possible: what happened, what you expected, and your device model.
