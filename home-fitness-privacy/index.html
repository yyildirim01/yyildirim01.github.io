Home Fitness: Home Workout

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Home Fitness: Home Workout ("Home Fitness", "the app", "we") handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Home Fitness does not require an account and does not collect or share personal data with the developer. The app is designed to provide workout programs, exercise guidance, fitness articles and recipes without asking you for personal information.

  • Workout activity and preferences: workout completion, minutes, estimated calories, plan progress and app preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Health and fitness information: the app does not request or collect medical records, precise health measurements or information from health-platform accounts.
  • Payment information: optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or payment details.

How Information Is Used

Locally stored workout activity and preferences are used only to show your progress and personalise your experience on your device. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for cross-app tracking.

Third-Party Services

Purchases and related store functionality are provided by Google Play and may be subject to Google's own privacy practices. You can review Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage and Deletion

App progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can remove this data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Purchase records remain managed by Google Play under your Google account.

Children's Privacy

Home Fitness is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Fitness and Health Notice

Home Fitness provides general fitness information and is not a medical service. The app does not diagnose conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
