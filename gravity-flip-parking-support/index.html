Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Gravity Flip Parking does not sell your personal data. Your game progress (coins, gems, unlocked cars, levels) stays on your device and never leaves it. If you submit an Endless Mode score, only the nickname you choose and your score are stored.

Why do I see ads?

The game shows ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads) via Google AdMob to keep the app free. Rewarded ads are always optional and give you a bonus (like a revive or extra coins) in exchange for watching.

I lost my progress after reinstalling the app. Can I get it back?

Game progress is stored locally on your device, not in the cloud. Reinstalling or switching devices does not carry your progress over. We're sorry for the inconvenience!

My rewarded ad didn't load / show. What do I do?

Ad availability depends on your network connection and ad inventory at that moment. Try again after a few seconds, or check your internet connection. If the problem continues, contact us with your device model.

How does the Endless Mode leaderboard work?

When you get a new best score in Endless Mode, you can choose a nickname and submit it to the online leaderboard. You can see how you rank against other players.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Send us an email using the button above with as much detail as possible (what happened, what you expected, and your device model).

Can I play Gravity Flip Parking offline?

Yes, the core game works offline. An internet connection is only needed for ads and the Endless Mode online leaderboard.
