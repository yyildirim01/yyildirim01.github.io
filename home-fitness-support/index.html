Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the right workout?

Select a program that matches your current fitness level and goal. Start with beginner-friendly sessions if you are new to exercise, and increase difficulty gradually.

Where is my workout progress stored?

Your completed workouts, minutes, estimated calories, plan progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Home Fitness does not require an account.

Are calorie values exact?

No. Calorie values are estimates for general guidance and can vary based on your body, intensity, technique and other factors.

How do I restore an in-app purchase?

Use the restore-purchase option in the app, while signed in with the same Google account used for the original purchase. If the purchase is still unavailable, contact us.

Can I use Home Fitness offline?

Core workout features can be used without a continuous connection after app content has loaded. Google Play purchase and store functions require an internet connection.

Is Home Fitness medical advice?

No. Home Fitness provides general fitness information only. Stop exercising if you feel pain or unwell, and consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a new exercise program when appropriate.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us using the button above. Include what happened, what you expected, your device model and Android version so we can investigate.

How does Home Fitness handle my data?

Read the Home Fitness Privacy Policy for details.

Safety reminder: Use proper form, choose a suitable difficulty level and make sure your training area is safe. The app does not replace professional medical or fitness advice.
